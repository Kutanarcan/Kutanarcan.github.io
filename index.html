Cube Roll Puzzle

My current project: an open-source hybrid-casual puzzle game, built stage by stage.

A full hybrid-casual process, start to finish

I'm building Cube Roll Puzzle to experience the whole hybrid-casual journey myself, from the first prototype to LiveOps. In past roles I worked on parts of that path; here I own every stage.

Each stage has to answer one question before the next begins, and every step forward is a written go, iterate, pivot or kill decision. The market and player data are simulated; the development and the process are real.

The production core is pure C#, tested without opening Unity, with a thin view layer on top. AI works as a pair programmer under rule sets I wrote, and every phase is reviewed by hand.
